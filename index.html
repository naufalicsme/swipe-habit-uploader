Swipe Habit Uploader

Swipe Habit Uploader is a private, internal publishing tool used only by the owner of the Swipe Habit YouTube channel, a channel of short, satisfying ASMR videos.

What it does

  • Uploads finished Shorts from the owner's computer to the owner's own YouTube channel.
  • Sets each video's title, description, tags, publish schedule, "made for kids" status and altered/synthetic content disclosure.
  • Reads basic information about the owner's own channel to confirm it is uploading to the right channel.

Who can use it

The tool is not offered to the public. It runs only on the channel owner's computer and is authorised only for the channel owner's own Google account. It does not access, collect or store any data about other YouTube users or viewers.

YouTube API Services

This tool uses YouTube API Services. By using it you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Data is handled according to our Privacy Policy and the Google Privacy Policy.
